# Digital Footprint Checker

Your **digital footprint** includes public accounts, posts, and other traces associated with handles you have used online.

## Check your own footprint

1. List usernames you previously used.
2. Search them with the [digital footprint checker](https://whatsmynameapps.us/digital-footprint-check/).
3. Open matching profiles to confirm they belong to you.
4. Review privacy settings and remove information you no longer want public.
5. Close unused accounts through each platform's official account settings, where available.

A username search cannot find every trace of an online identity and does not remove information from third-party websites.

For more, see [username search](username-search.md).
